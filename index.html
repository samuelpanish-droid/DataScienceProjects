Featured · Classification · Business impact

Customer Churn Prediction & Revenue Impact

Telecom companies lose revenue every time a customer cancels. Using 7,043 IBM Telco customers, I built a leak-free scikit-learn pipeline, compared logistic regression with a random forest, then ranked customers by risk to size a targeted retention campaign.

Logistic regression won on balance (ROC-AUC 0.845, F1 0.631). The random forest caught more churners but flagged too many loyal customers to be cost-effective.

Variance threshold beat the baseline (R² 0.656 vs 0.643). Five χ² features kept 97.4% mushroom accuracy.

Week 7 · LoRA · Efficient fine-tuning

Instruction Fine-Tuning Mistral with LoRA

Fine-tunes Mistral-7B-Instruct on 1,000 Dolly examples using 4-bit quantization and LoRA adapters, training only a small fraction of the model's parameters.
